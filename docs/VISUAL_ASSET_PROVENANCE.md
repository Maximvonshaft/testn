# Visual asset provenance and claim boundary

The scene-state matrices in `public/assets/visual/` are controlled digital visualisations produced for the AQUASTONE website interaction. They are not photographs of completed AQUASTONE projects and are not laboratory measurements of physical samples.

## Permitted use

- brand and application visualisation;
- interaction and responsive acceptance;
- design-direction review;
- project discussion before physical sample approval.

## Prohibited representation

The assets must not be represented as:

- exact colour matches to a supplied physical sample;
- installed-project photography;
- measured roughness, gloss, height or reflectance data;
- certification or performance evidence.

## Calibration replacement contract

Once controlled sample photography or scanning is available, replace the material sources in the offline renderer while preserving:

- system IDs;
- material IDs and matrix order;
- desktop/mobile frame dimensions;
- file names and public URLs.

This keeps the website and QA contract stable while improving product accuracy.
