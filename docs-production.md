# AQUASTONE Launch Acceptance Contract

## Technical acceptance

- Locked dependency graph
- Zero high-severity production dependency findings
- Astro/TypeScript strict checks pass
- Production Worker build passes
- Static asset and claim-integrity audit passes
- Chromium, Firefox, WebKit, Mobile Chrome and Mobile Safari acceptance passes
- No page-level horizontal overflow
- Scene and material states synchronize
- Five-layer interaction remains available with WebGL fallback
- Axe reports no serious accessibility violations
- API rejects unsupported methods, invalid payloads and cross-origin submissions
- Lead collection fails closed without Turnstile and delivery secrets

## Business-source launch dependencies

Public lead collection must remain disabled until the responsible business owner supplies and verifies:

- legal entity and privacy-controller details;
- production domain;
- recipient CRM/webhook;
- Turnstile credentials;
- certification, declaration and test references;
- customer-facing contact and response ownership;
- warranty, MOQ, lead-time and commercial terms where published.
